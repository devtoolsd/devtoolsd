SEMAPRAX keeps human-readable `.spx` source as its canonical Git projection
while exposing deterministic, versioned semantic graphs and revision-bound
semantic patches for agents. Its pre-alpha compiler includes bounded native
C11/Clang and WebAssembly Core lanes; the project documents implemented
evidence separately from its broader research roadmap.
