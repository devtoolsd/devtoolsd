ABAP (Advanced Business Application Programming) runs inside the SAP NetWeaver application server and powers the business logic of SAP ERP, S/4HANA, and related enterprise systems. With an installed base spanning most large enterprises worldwide, there are millions of lines of ABAP in production. Modern ABAP supports object-oriented programming, RESTful APIs, and cloud deployment.
