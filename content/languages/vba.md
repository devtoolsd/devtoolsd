VBA is embedded in every copy of Microsoft Office and remains the primary tool for automating complex Excel spreadsheets, Word document generation, and Access database workflows in enterprise environments. Despite its age, it processes billions of dollars in financial calculations daily through Excel macros that have run untouched for decades.
