COBOL was designed to be readable by non-programmers and to process business records at scale. Roughly 95% of ATM transactions and the majority of banking core systems worldwide still run on COBOL. Billions of lines of COBOL code run on IBM mainframes, and skilled COBOL developers remain in high demand.
