Erlang was built by Ericsson to power telephone switches that could not go down. Its actor model, supervision trees, and hot code reloading enable systems with "nine nines" (99.9999999%) uptime. WhatsApp ran on Erlang and scaled to 2 million concurrent connections per server. Elixir inherits the BEAM VM and all its reliability guarantees.
