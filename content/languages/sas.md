SAS combines a procedural programming language with integrated statistical procedures for data management and analysis. It holds a dominant position in pharmaceutical clinical trials, banking risk analysis, and government statistics — sectors where regulatory requirements mandate validated, reproducible analysis environments. The SAS data step and PROC SQL remain foundational for millions of analysts.
