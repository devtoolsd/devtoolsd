Perl's "there's more than one way to do it" philosophy and unmatched regular expression integration made it the dominant glue language of the early web. It remains heavily used in bioinformatics (BioPerl), legacy systems administration, and anywhere dense text transformation is required. CPAN, Perl's package repository, contains over 200,000 modules.
