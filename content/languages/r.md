R was built by statisticians for statistics and remains the dominant language in academia, biostatistics, and quantitative research. The tidyverse ecosystem (ggplot2, dplyr, tidyr) provides a coherent, expressive grammar for data manipulation and visualisation. R Shiny enables interactive web-based dashboards from pure R code.
