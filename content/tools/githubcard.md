GitHubCard builds cards from public GitHub data. Profile cards show stats, contribution streaks, top languages and repositories; repository cards show stars, forks, languages, contributors, star history and commit activity. Embed live cards at `https://githubcard.com/{username}.svg` or `https://githubcard.com/{owner}/{repo}.svg`, or export SVG/PNG. Free to use; Pro is a one-time purchase for premium themes and watermark-free exports.
