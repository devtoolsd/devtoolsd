CoworkingView publishes a keyless REST API at [coworkingview.com/en/api](https://coworkingview.com/en/api) and open clients in [`coworkingview/flex-office-data`](https://github.com/coworkingview/flex-office-data) (MIT). The clients read operator-published monthly list prices for coworking desks and private offices in European cities. The Price Index figures themselves are licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) and are also available at [coworkingview.com/en/tools/price-index](https://coworkingview.com/en/tools/price-index).
