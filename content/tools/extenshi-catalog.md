long_description: |
  Extenshi Catalog indexes browser extensions from the Chrome Web Store, Firefox Add-ons, and the Edge Add-ons store in one place and scans each one automatically. Every extension has a report with a safety score, a severity breakdown of its findings, an analysis of the permissions it requests, and how it compares with others in its category. You can search and filter across all three stores by permission, category, risk, and store, paste a store link for an on-demand verdict on something you are about to install, and follow an extension's history as new versions are published and rescanned. Extension authors can claim their own listing by proving ownership through a DNS record, the manifest email, or an email PIN, then respond to findings raised against their extension and complete the data declaration describing what it collects.
