rdyrct creates short links and QR codes with campaign analytics that do not store IP addresses. It supports branded domains, UTM tagging, organizations with role-based access, scoped API keys and MCP access through OAuth.

It has a free hosted plan and can be self-hosted on Cloudflare Workers, D1, KV and R2 under the MIT license.
