Cloady hosts applications and supporting services in a shared workspace. Developers can deploy from source or container images, configure environment variables, attach persistent storage, and inspect logs without administering the underlying server OS.

The hosted platform is proprietary and offers a free workspace allowance alongside paid resource allocation. Its MCP connector supports OAuth for compatible AI clients.
