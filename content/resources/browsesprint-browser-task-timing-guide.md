BrowseSprint's guide explains how to define a browser task's expected outcome,
record three baseline runs and three changed runs, and compare correctness
alongside elapsed time. The blank CSV records session state, success, tool calls,
retries and timing fields. The guide and worksheet are available without signup.

- [Download the blank CSV worksheet](https://codexbrowse.site/browser-task-benchmark.csv)
- [Editable English and Chinese resource pack](https://github.com/liubrain39/browsesprint-resources)

BrowseSprint is an independent research project. No browser extension is released,
and the guide does not claim a verified speedup or an affiliation with OpenAI.
