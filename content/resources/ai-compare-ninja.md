AI Compare Ninja is a free editorial resource for researching AI software, comparing provider-sourced prices, plans, limits and alternatives, and planning subscription or LLM API costs. It is available without registration in eight languages.

The API calculator estimates costs from request volume, input/output-token assumptions and published OpenRouter route prices. Estimates are not invoices or API access. The prompt checker is rule-based. Image metadata and Content Credentials do not prove authenticity or the absence of AI. Assistant and loop pages provide instructions for the reader’s chosen tools; no hosted agent execution, inference or models are supplied.

Configurable embeds are available for the calculators and selected resources. The site also offers an optional newsletter and email-confirmed price alerts. Links labelled “Advertisement*” may be advertising or affiliate links; catalogue positions are not sold.
