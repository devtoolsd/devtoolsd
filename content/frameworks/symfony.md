Symfony is both a full-stack framework and a collection of standalone PHP components — HttpFoundation, Console, DependencyInjection, EventDispatcher — that form the plumbing of the PHP ecosystem. Laravel, Drupal, Magento, and phpBB are all built on Symfony components.
