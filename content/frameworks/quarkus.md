Quarkus was built for containers — it starts in milliseconds and uses a fraction of the memory of traditional Spring Boot apps when compiled to native with GraalVM. It is developer-friendly with live reload, unified configuration, and familiar extensions for JPA, RESTEasy, Kafka, and more.
